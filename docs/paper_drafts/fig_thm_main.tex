\caption{\textbf{Empirical verification of Theorem~\ref{thm:stability}.}
On Qwen2.5-0.5B + LoRA with probe size $2{,}000$ and constant
$\eta = 2\times 10^{-4}$, the measured \textsc{TADS} anchor
displacement (red; App.~F cell~D, median over $24$ decoder layers,
$68$ post-warmup refresh points, rescaled $\times 14$ for
visibility) stays strictly below the empirical bound
$(2\hat{C}_\Sigma / \gamma_{\min})\eta = 46.24$ (blue dashed; shaded
safe region) at every refresh point -- median tightness
$1.46\times 10^{-3}$, worst-case $7.4\times 10^{-3}$
($\hat{C}_\Sigma = 5.78\times 10^{3}$, $\gamma_{\min} = 0.050$).
The matched no-anchor consensus control (grey dotted; same
instrumentation, \texttt{use\_anchor=false}, \texttt{tads.lam=0},
otherwise identical to cell~D; $23$ of $68$ refresh points
sub-sampled for display, \emph{same} $\times 14$ rescale) is
uncontrolled: $68/68$ measured medians exceed the \textsc{TADS}-D
median ($d=0.068$); $6$ of the $23$ shown medians cross the bound
itself (circled), and the worst single layer-step reaches $1.998$
($5.8\times$ \textsc{TADS}'s worst, off-chart at $27.97$ rescaled).
The two curves share the same vertical scale and their floors
overlap (the control dips into the \textsc{TADS} band repeatedly);
the contrast is in \emph{volatility and boundedness}, not in a
constant offset. Full diagnostics in
App.~\ref{app:thm-verification}.}
